Most people meet beacon because something broke: `json.loads` threw on a model reply, a tool call came back as a string, a schema error was unreadable. These recipes are organized by **symptom** — the thing you saw — not by block name. Each one is the same four sections:

- **Symptom** — the error or behaviour you actually hit.
- **Minimal fix** — the smallest change that solves it.
- **What it guarantees** — what you can now rely on.
- **What it does not do** — the honest edge, so you know where the block stops.

That last section is deliberate. A boundary block is only trustworthy if you know exactly where its job ends.

## Find your symptom

| --- | --- | --- |

| Show array results as they stream in | [Show results as they stream in](../stream-results/) | `stream_json` |

Every recipe links to the block's full reference for the rest of the options.
